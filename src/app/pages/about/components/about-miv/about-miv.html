ABOUT MIV MISSION

A Heart for Mission. A Commitment to Transformation.

MIV Mission is committed to advancing God's Kingdom and making a meaningful difference in the lives of people and communities through mission-focused initiatives.

Our work is centred on reaching people, supporting mission fields, equipping individuals for effective service, and mobilising resources to make lasting impact.

Our Vision

To see lives transformed and communities impacted through the advancement of God's Kingdom.

Our Mission

To reach, equip, support, and mobilise people for meaningful mission work and service.
